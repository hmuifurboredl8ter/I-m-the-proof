What to send

The trilogy runs on evidence, not promises, so the best suggestions show how they fit that standard.

  • A new track. A fourth opponent or a different failure of language, with the verdict it would end on.
  • A hook or line angle. A way to sharpen an accusation, a verdict, or the moment the beat drops out.
  • Beat or production. A tempo, texture, or arrangement idea, such as where silence should do the work.
  • A visual for the lyric displays. A color, motion, or scene idea for any of the three tracks.
