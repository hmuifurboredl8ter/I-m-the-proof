You keep describing yourself. I keep producing evidence.

Three songs. Three opponents. One standard: when language meets reality, what remains standing?

Play the trilogy

I'm the Proof is a three-part rap trilogy about the difference between what people claim and what their actions reveal. Each track puts a different kind of failure on trial: broken promises, reckless talk, and attention disguised as hate. Explore the lyrics, follow the escalation, and judge the evidence.

  1. You the Promise

    Words abandoned

    A broken promise changes the meaning of everything that came before it. This track confronts the damage left behind when someone's words fail to become actions. A promise points toward the future. Proof records what actually happened.

    83.35 BPM. Broad declarations build the case. Short verdicts bring it to a halt.

    Open lyric display

  2. You the Talk

    Words that backfire

    A rival keeps warning a woman about the speaker. Every warning gives her another reason to get curious. Fear becomes attention, attention becomes interest, and the attempt to discredit someone turns into unpaid publicity.

    86.13 BPM. Clauses spill forward like a confrontation unfolding in real time.

    Open lyric display

  3. You the Fan

    Attention exposed

    At a gas station, three men whisper while a woman watches. Their hostility reveals how much attention they give the person they claim to dismiss. She crosses the space, asks who he is, hears the music, and makes up her own mind.

    86.13 BPM. A hard drill bounce gives way to a beatless bridge, where silence puts every claim under examination.

    Open lyric display

One escalation

The standard never changes. The opposing identity fails in a different way with every track.

Promise

You failed to become your words. Proof means staying, rebuilding, and carrying the consequences someone else left behind.

Talk

Your words now work for me. Every repetition strengthens the reputation you meant to destroy.

Fan

Your attention tells the truth. Watching, whispering, and repeating a name turns hostility into involuntary promotion.

warning + repetition → curiosity
curiosity + restraint → backfire

The witness

Across the trilogy, the woman's role changes. In Promise, she carries the damage. In Talk, she tests the warning. In Fan, she judges the scene for herself.

She is not a prize at the end of a contest. She is the witness who makes every claim answerable to reality.

Not a demand to be believed. A record of what happens when talk meets consequence.
